# Generation prompt system

## Common prompt

Create a fictional “Crafting Europe” euro banknote concept-validation plate, not a reproduction of an existing banknote. Use a horizontal full-bleed canvas with straight edges, viewed perfectly front-on, with no outer margin, rotation, perspective distortion, shadow, or surrounding background.

The central image must remain unmistakably Minecraft-native through canonical or near-canonical textures, terrain, proportions, vegetation, workstations, objects, and mechanisms. Avoid continuous dark outlines around cubes. Preserve direct Minecraft imagery while integrating restrained guilloche, paper tone, fine linework, subtle overprint, selective engraving, and mild ink-like saturation.

Use a stable family architecture: left value field with a dominant Minecraft-derived pixel numeral, smaller value, and euro symbol; broad central scene; and right institutional field with an integrated European flag of exactly twelve stars, borderless Europe map, signature reservation, secondary value, and the exact text `EURO ΕΥΡΩ ЕВРО`. Add no game logo, interface elements, pseudo-text, extra words, recipe panels, explanatory arrows, generic voxel-fantasy styling, glossy CGI, bloom, or photographic setting.

Every front uses a single natural landscape or cave view and an offset circular near-white ore label containing only one centered canonical isometric ore block. Every back uses an elevated oblique spectator-like view, close enough that canonical objects remain identifiable, and one dominant Minecraft-native constructed subject.
